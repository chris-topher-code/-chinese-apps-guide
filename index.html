Quick comparisons

Which one should I use?

★ Strong payment recommendation

Link a Wise or Revolut card to both WeChat and Alipay before you arrive.

This is the single most important payment step for foreigners. A Wise or Revolut card lets you spend directly from your foreign account at QR payment terminals across China. Link the same card in WeChat Pay AND Alipay so you can pay almost anywhere — even at shops that accept only one of them. Test a small payment before you rely on it.

WeChat Pay vs Alipay vs TenPayGo

Use WeChat Pay and Alipay together if possible: WeChat Pay is everywhere socially, while Alipay is often smoother for travel, utilities, and foreign card setup. TenPayGo is the new Tencent option for visitors who want to pay without a WeChat account.

Taobao vs JD vs Pinduoduo

Taobao has everything, JD is stronger for reliable electronics and delivery, Pinduoduo is deal-heavy but harder for beginners.

Amap vs Didi

Amap is the map and transit brain. Didi is the ride-hailing backup when public transport is not enough.

Connectivity

VPNs that work in China (2026)

Install and test a paid VPN before you fly. App stores and VPN sites are often blocked inside China. Mullvad is the recommended pick; Astrill works but is slow and expensive; Surfshark works with No Border mode enabled.

Mullvad — recommended

Privacy-focused and the most reliable pick for 2026.

Astrill — slow + expensive

China-focused and reliable at connecting, but plans are costly and speeds can be slow.

Surfshark — No Border mode

Affordable option that works when No Border mode is enabled.

VPN use is legally restricted in China; check current rules and use responsibly.

Before you land

First-week tips

Install WeChat, Alipay, a map app, and a translation app before landing. Payment and identity rules change, so verify setup requirements in the app before relying on it.

Install before landing

Download WeChat, Alipay, Amap, and a translation app while you still have fast, unrestricted Wi-Fi.

Set up payments early

Link a Wise or Revolut card in both WeChat Pay and Alipay, then test a small QR payment before depending on it.

Save addresses in Chinese

Keep your hotel, school, or apartment address in Chinese so taxi drivers and delivery couriers can find you.

Stay connected

Arrange roaming or a China eSIM before you fly, and carry a power bank for long days out.

Power bank warning

Power banks without CCC certification are not allowed on flights in China. Airport security confiscates them permanently — even expensive ones. Check for the CCC mark before you fly.
